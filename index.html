FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is PDFNova really free?
Yes — every tool is completely free with no usage limits, no signup and no hidden premium tiers.
Are my files uploaded to a server?
No. PDFNova processes everything locally in your browser using modern client-side libraries. Your files never leave your device, which makes it ideal for confidential documents, tax forms, and NDAs.
Is there a file size limit?
There is no artificial limit. Practical limits depend on your device's memory — most documents up to a few hundred megabytes work seamlessly on a modern computer or phone.
Which formats can I convert?
You can convert PDF to Word (.docx), Excel (.csv/.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx), JPG, PNG, and Text. You can also convert Word, Excel, PowerPoint, JPG, PNG, HTML, and camera scans into PDF!
Does it work on my phone?
Absolutely. The whole site is responsive and touch-friendly — you can sign, merge, compress, or scan a document straight from your phone's browser.
How does the OCR tool work?
Our OCR (Optical Character Recognition) runs directly in your browser using WebAssembly technology. It inspects scanned PDFs and photos, identifies characters, and produces selectable, copyable text with zero data sent across the internet.
